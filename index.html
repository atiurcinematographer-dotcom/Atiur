About

Mohammad Atiur Rahman

Documentary Filmmaker • Cinematographer • Editor

I am an independent Bangladeshi documentary filmmaker dedicated to telling authentic stories about people, culture, traditions, heritage and forgotten places through cinematic storytelling.

Every film is an attempt to preserve memories, inspire audiences and reveal the unseen beauty of Bangladesh.
